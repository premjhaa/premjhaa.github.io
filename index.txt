    <p>His writing is shaped by an interest in human psychology, philosophy, personal experiences, and the gap between what people express publicly and what they carry privately.         <h3>Pages I Never Sent</h3>
        <p class="tagline">Poems of the words I never had the courage to send.</p>
        <p><em>Pages I Never Sent</em> is a collection of poems born from the spaces between words - the letters left unsaid, the feelings hidden too well, and the goodbyes that never found their voice. Each page is a quiet confession, a memory, a wound, and a prayer.</p>
        <p>This book is for anyone who has loved deeply, lost silently, overthought endlessly, and written in the dark just to stay sane. These poems are not answers. They are pieces of the heart he had to let go of.</p>
        <p>If you've ever written something you never sent, this book is for you.</p>
