About Prem Jha – Indian Author and Poet

Prem Jha is an Indian author and poet based in Chandigarh, India. He is the author of Pages I Never Sent, his debut poetry collection exploring memories, solitude, relationships and the emotions that often remain unspoken.

His writing explores human psychology, philosophy and personal experiences. Through his work, he seeks to give expression to thoughts and feelings that often remain unspoken.

Prem is building his identity as an independent author and writer, sharing his literary work through his official website and various digital publications.
